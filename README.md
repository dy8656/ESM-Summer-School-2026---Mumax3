# MuMax3 Tutorial: Simulating Magnetic Textures and Dynamics

Micromagnetic simulations have become an indispensable tool for understanding and predicting magnetic phenomena across a wide range of length and time scales. This hands-on tutorial provides an introduction to the GPU-accelerated micromagnetic simulation package MuMax3, combining the underlying physical concepts with practical experience in setting up and analyzing simulations.

After a brief review of micromagnetic theory and an introduction to the MuMax3 scripting language, simulation workflow, and key input parameters, participants will work through three representative examples. The tutorial covers (i) the formation of magnetic ground states as a function of the simulation geometry, including the emergence of vortex states, (ii) the current-driven dynamics of a magnetic skyrmion and the analysis of its motion, and (iii) the calculation of magnetic hysteresis loops under an external magnetic field. Each example is accompanied by guided exercises that encourage participants to explore the influence of material parameters and simulation settings.

The tutorial is aimed at students and researchers who are new to micromagnetic simulations and will provide the practical skills needed to perform and interpret MuMax3 simulations for contemporary problems in magnetism.
